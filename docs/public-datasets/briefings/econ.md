# Data Inventory
- `raw/bea/CAEMP25N.zip`: Bureau of Economic Analysis Local Area Personal Income and Employment (CAEMP25N) ZIP downloaded 2026-05-28. Provider-native 60-file bundle covering U.S. national, county, metropolitan, and combined statistical area geographies with 33 NAICS industry lines. Annual employment, wage, and average earnings measures for 2001-2022; 104,874 rows in the all-areas table; units in number of jobs and current-dollar compensation. ZIP is stored unmodified for full provenance.
- `raw/bea/CAGDP2.zip`: BEA Gross Domestic Product by County (CAGDP2) ZIP downloaded 2026-05-29. Archive contains 55 CSV tables spanning national totals, 50 states and DC, county and metropolitan/micropolitan areas with GeoFIPS and LineCode keys. Annual GDP, personal income, and compensation values for 2001-2024 in thousands of current dollars; the all-areas table holds 3,218 county and aggregate records per series across 24 year columns. Metadata XML and footnote HTML retained from provider package.
- `raw/bea/CAINC1.zip`: BEA CAINC1 state personal income summary ZIP (annual, 1969-2024). Contains national and state CSV slices plus metadata; original layout preserved; supports state-level income, per-capita, and population benchmarks.
- `raw/census/acs/csv_pus.zip`: 2024 ACS 1-year Public Use Microdata Sample combined person and housing CSV package (~603 MB). PUMS geography at PUMA level with full microdata fields; contents remain compressed pending downstream extraction.
- `raw/census/cps/jan26pub.zip`: January 2026 Current Population Survey basic monthly public-use file ZIP (~9 MB). Includes person-level labor force microdata with national coverage; stored as-received.
- `raw/census/ahs/AHS_2023_National_PUF_v1.1_CSV.zip`: 2023 American Housing Survey national public-use CSV bundle (~129 MB). Delivers household and unit-level housing characteristics with survey weights; provider archive retained intact.
- `raw/fhfa/hpi_at_state.csv` & `raw/fhfa/hpi_at_metro.csv`: Federal Housing Finance Agency all-transactions House Price Index quarterly CSVs through 2025Q4. State file contains 10,403 rows across 51 geographies; metro file covers 83,639 rows for 410 MSAs. Units are index values (1980Q1=100) with percentage change columns per provider schema.
- `raw/fred/UNRATE.csv`, `raw/fred/CPIAUCSL.csv`, `raw/fred/FEDFUNDS.csv`, `raw/fred/DGS10.csv`, `raw/fred/GDP.csv`, `raw/fred/IC4WSA.csv`: Federal Reserve Economic Data national time series in provider CSV format. Coverage spans 1947-2026 monthly CPI, 1948-2026 monthly unemployment, 1954-2026 monthly federal funds, 1962-2026 daily 10-year Treasury yields (16,787 observations), 1947-2026 quarterly nominal GDP, and 1967-2026 weekly initial unemployment claims. Each file follows FRED's two-column schema (`observation_date`, value) with U.S. national scope.
- `raw/fred/PAYEMS.csv`: FRED Total Nonfarm Payroll Employment series downloaded 2026-05-29 via `fredgraph` CSV endpoint. Monthly, seasonally adjusted national employment (thousands of persons) from 1939-01-01 through 2026-04-01; 1,048 observations with columns `observation_date` and `PAYEMS`. File stored exactly as provided.
- `raw/treasury/daily_treasury_yield_curve_2024_2026.csv`: Treasury Fiscal Data par yield curve aggregation for 1-month to 30-year maturities, daily from 2024-01-02 through 2026-05-08 (588 records). Includes maturity columns (`BC_*`) with percent yields and provenance fields (`source_file`, `Id`).
- `raw/treasury/operating_cash_balance.csv`: Daily Treasury Statement operating cash balance dataset (10,000 rows, 2014-06-04 through 2026-05-07). Columns capture account type, daily close/open balances, and fiscal/calendar metadata; units in millions of USD.
- `raw/census_bfs/bfs_us_apps_weekly_nsa.csv`: Census Business Formation Statistics national weekly non-seasonally-adjusted applications, 2006 W01 - 2026 W17 (1,060 rows). Measures BA, HBA, WBA, CBA counts plus year-over-year percentage deltas for U.S. totals.
- `raw/imf_weo/WEOApr2026all.xlsx`: International Monetary Fund World Economic Outlook April 2026 workbook (5.3 MB). Contains ~8,660 country-indicator rows across 197 economies plus aggregates, each with 1980-2029 annual columns covering GDP levels, inflation, fiscal balances, external accounts, and volume growth. Workbook retains provider tab structure, metadata columns (units, scale, subject descriptors), and projections through 2029; redistribution subject to IMF WEO license review.
- `raw/worldbank/cpi/API_USA_FP.CPI.TOTL.ZG_EN_csv_v2_13049.csv`: World Bank WDI CPI inflation (% change) for the United States, annual observations 1960-2024 with accompanying metadata CSVs retained in folder.

- `raw/zillow/City_zhvi_uc_sfr_tier_0.33_0.67_sm_sa_month.csv`: Zillow Home Value Index city-level smoothed, seasonally adjusted series (Jan 2000 - Mar 2026). Matrix layout across 21,410 regions and monthly value columns; license review flagged for downstream redistribution.
- `raw/bea_nipa/NIPA_Section1_Tables_April2024.xlsx`: BEA National Income and Product Accounts Section 1 workbook downloaded 2026-05-30. - `raw/worldbank/WDI_CSV_2026_04_09.zip`: World Bank World Development Indicators bulk CSV archive downloaded 2026-06-01. Annual coverage spans 1960-2025 across 266 economies and aggregate regions, covering macro indicators including GDP, inflation, trade, debt, education, and related development measures with values, units, scale factors, and footnotes. File stored unmodified under `raw/worldbank/`; license requires standard World Bank Data attribution.
- `raw/pwt/pwt100.xlsx`: Penn World Table version 10.0 workbook downloaded 2026-06-01. Provider Excel package retains `Data`, `Info`, and `Legend` sheets with 52 fields covering real GDP (expenditure/output-side, chained 2017 USD), capital stock, employment, hours, human capital, prices, total factor productivity, and demand aggregates. `Data` tab holds 12,810 country-year observations for 183 economies and regions spanning 1950-2019 with country ISO codes and annual frequency; population and employment values are in millions, GDP aggregates in millions of 2017 USD PPP, capital in current PPP millions, and productivity indexes relative to the United States. Workbook stored exactly as provided for reproducibility.
- `raw/census_cbp/cbp22co.zip`: Census County Business Patterns 2022 county ZIP downloaded 2026-06-01. Provider package retains native `cbp22co.txt` with 1,100,804 county-industry rows spanning 3,192 counties and county-equivalents by NAICS 2-6 digit industry (1,990 industry codes). Fields cover establishments (`EST`), paid employees (`EMP`), first-quarter payroll (`PAYQTR1`), annual payroll (`PAYANN`), employment-size class counts, and suppression flags. Measures represent 2022 annual totals in number of firms, employees, and USD payroll; encrypted or suppressed cells follow Census disclosure rules. Archive stored intact for provenance and layout documentation.
