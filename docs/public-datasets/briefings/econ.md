# Coverage Roadmap

This dataset is a broad raw economics substrate, not a completed claim that every economist need is already covered. The 2026-06-01 capacity and inode-hotspot audits found that inode pressure was dominated by `.remote-agent` automation cache/workspace trees rather than provider-native `raw/` economics data. Cleanup run `2d9fe31b-2951-4c52-afe6-f2c5f21f78cb` removed the stale cache targets and recovered about 90,249 free inodes, so small controlled ingestion can resume with inode monitoring.

Highest-value missing or incomplete public-source families:

- Macro/vintage data: ALFRED vintages, additional Federal Reserve statistical releases, ECB SDW, ONS bulk data, and richer NBER business-cycle/source metadata.
- Labor, household, and demographic microdata: CE PUMD 2024 and a 2024 NCES IPEDS postsecondary core slice are now present, but longer CPS/ACS/SIPP vintages, CE historical/state-weight supplements, IPUMS extracts where credentials and redistribution terms permit, PSID, GSS, SSA public-use files, broader NCES K-12/longitudinal products, and restricted-use education/labor inputs remain incomplete.
- Public finance, tax, and transfer systems: Treasury debt/auction Fiscal Data, IRS Statistics of Income ZIP data, Census 2023 state/local government finance public-use records, and CMS National Health Expenditure Accounts are now present, but TreasuryDirect XML/history supplements, SSA/OASDI tables, restricted CMS claims/provider files, historical Census government-finance vintages, and deeper state/local fiscal archives remain incomplete.
- Firm, industry, and market structure: SEC EDGAR companyfacts/submissions bulk archives and Census SUSB 2022 establishment-industry tables are now present, but Census ABS/ASE owner-demographic data, historical SUSB/CBP vintages, BLS BED/QCEW history, OpenCorporates where license permits, full-text EDGAR filings, private-company datasets, and licensed WRDS/CRSP/Compustat references remain incomplete.
- Credit, banking, mortgages, and financial markets: FDIC BankFind latest-quarter bank financials/institutions are now present, but additional HMDA vintages, additional CFPB consumer-credit datasets, historical FFIEC Call Report bulk archives, FR Y-9C holding-company reports, broader CFTC Commitments of Traders history, additional BIS/IMF financial series, and market reference data where license allows remain incomplete.
- Housing and real estate: Redfin, NAR, Case-Shiller, Freddie Mac AIMI, Fannie Mae surveys, Apartment List rents, HMDA, building permits, and expanded AHS/HUD products.
- International trade, agriculture, and development: USDA NASS QuickStats economics and 2022 Census of Agriculture bulk extracts plus FAOSTAT are now present, but UN Comtrade, WTO, remaining NASS QuickStats categories, USDA ERS, ILOSTAT, additional IMF datasets, Eurostat/ECB breadth, and more World Bank subject databases remain incomplete.
